1/ You are worthy, because you are a human.

2/ Solve problems by creating new knowledge.

3/ Knowledge creation is a hard (not easy) yet the process issimple, governed by the loop: 
1. Identify an important problem (important to you)
2. Conjecture: Invent an explainatory theory to solve the problem
3. Criticize: Attempt hard to falsify the conjecture
4. Accept the conjecture as a new explainatory theory if it passes criticisms, else iterate through the next cycle

>On a side note even though you fail, you did create new knowledge; which means you have a better chance at next attempt and better chance at solving problems.

4/ Follow your curiosity, even though your brain keeps exploding with ideas — it's not enough; an idea is just the starting point, it's just a new conjecture — you need to iterate through to complete the loop and create new useful knowledge.

5/ Work Hard and become excellent! *Excellence is the quality to take the pain*
- Go to a cafe. Drink coffee. Make Great Things. Work on important problems. *Important to you!* Ship, put things out in the world. 
- Work/Talk/Listen with/to/about excellent engineers and makers of things.
- Be obsessed about solving the problem - because you are limited by time, thus make sure you're picking the most important problem and spending incredible amount of time running the loop.
- Have fun - cultivate the environment so that all types of idea creation align: explicit, implicit and unconcious.
- Run

6/ Don't conform to authority, people around you or anyone, form your own world view and think for yourself.

7/ Disobidience is not a character flaw, it's a quality to embrace, if you're not disobidient — you'll follow the path of least resistance set by an authority figure (a human, your own mind, temptations or worse a program).

Another way to think about it is - Don't be superstitious, that's the least reistant path, if you don't have an explainatory theory - you're being superstitious, always follow your best explainatory theories; if you don't have one create it and follow the loop.

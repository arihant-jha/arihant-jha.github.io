If you don't pick the most important thing at the moment — this can become the source of regret.

How to know what's most important?

Imagine the possibilities and then compare them

**Possibility 1** — I'm able to solve an IMO problem, that looks something like this:

![[Pasted image 20260803144858.png]]

**Possibility 2** — 100 businesses are using FlexyOps to run their business, creating new knowledge and knowledge is being disseminated and helping other businesses.

**Possibility 3** — I have made a UAV Drone and I'm able to control it.

> Possibility 2 !!!

---

But this doesn't mean to not be curious about other things, curiosity has been my biggest leverage; not just in 1 field, but in a lot of fields — which has helped me to pull idea from 1 field put it into another, etc. which is how a lot of discoveries also happened in the past. But as Paul Graham says distribute attention using a power law.

> the nth most important topic (m-1)/m^n of your attention, for some m > 1.

If I'm interested in 10 things -> most important thing gets 90% attention.

if 5 things -> 80%

if 2 things -> 50%

More things I'm interested in, more attention should go to the most important thing (not less!), and I'm interested in a lot of thigns.

---

How to do it?

$\epsilon=4$

Just do it, Paul Graham also has this great line in his essay `Life is short`:

> Cultivate a habit of impatience about the things you most want to do. Don't wait before climbing that mountain or writing that book or visiting your mother. You don't need to be constantly reminding yourself why you shouldn't wait. Just don't wait.

---

Every point in time it seems like, this moment or a time like this will stay forever; but it doesn't, it changes; can change for the good or for bad; so make sure to do the things in your control that you most want to do, don't think about them, do them!

---

Boldly chase outlier ideas!

Spend contiguous hours working on the same problem! -> this is the unit of great work.

Cultivate your environment, cultivate what goes in your head; the larger part of my brain(computer) is the unconcious part generating inexplicit and unconcious ideas; so work hard in contiguous hours, read, consume and do stuff that is towards that mission. Don't force yourself to remember thigns, it might not even be necessary, just work and cultivate the environment and there is a good chance you would have created a mountain of inexplicit ideas and unconcious ideas just by your actions than you could have by reading, writing and trying to remember the things.

Of course I need to explicitly state ideas to communicate, but that is still a small part of the process; I don't need to communicate everything!

A lot of people in history just did things and made stuff, after they died people studied their work and figured out explicit theories.. so the important part is not coming up with explicit ideas, but the [fun] process itself; communication doesn't just happen by explicit ideas, it happens through the produce of work as well — the software product, the hardware product, writing, mathematicians proof, spot on debate/speech — thus don't focus on communicating your explicit ideas too much; just work on the most important thigns, cultivate your environment, move towards the mission.

Agency > Intelligence

What is Agency?

It's the ability to take initiative, make decisions, and act effectively. Being proactive and capable of execution is what matters.

1/ Keep asking the questions — Am I working on, what I most want to work on? As you get older this gets increasingly dangerous if the answer is "no".

2/ Great Work involves spending what would seem to other people unreasonable amount of time on a problem.

3/ Tip of the spear focus — Always identify and attack the biggest limiter, don't spread efforts across secondary problems; laser in on single constraint if removed will unlock everything downstream.

4/ Burn the boats — it unlocks the ability to generate new ideas, come up with solutions, demon slayer, my own experience guides me towards this. No one is coming to save you.

5/ Something that grows exponentially becomes so valuable that it's worth making an extraordinary effort to get started.

6/ Frontal attack(contiguous hours of work) and rest of the time side attack(walking, shower, lying in bed). Frontal attack feeds questions to the later, so there is no point in doing 2nd if 1st is not being done. Relates back to the idea of following powerlaw in most important things.

7/ Aim to be the best and cultivate taste from the best in the field.

Conciously cultivate your taste in the work done in your field. Until you know which is the best and what makes it so, you don't know what you're aiming for.

That's so true, apple had — `Edwin land` and `Braun`.

8/ Don't "try to" — that's affectation; instead "be earnest" (showing sincere(serious) and intense conviction).

Be intellectually honest, you're trying to see more truth than others have seen so far.

Another component of earnestness is `informality`: focusing on things that matters instead of what doesn't.

Be the one who puts things out there.

> Don't bother too much about how you did it, or how you're going to do it. JUST DO IT AND KEEP DOING IT!

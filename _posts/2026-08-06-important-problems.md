## Wake up problem [Solved]

It's hard to wake up, brain sometimes give reasons to not, eyes don't open up, I'm sore from last day's 10k, sleep a little more - it's still early.

Based on evidence I've collected over 26 years and after reading about a lot of people, I have realized — **I'm Lazy** and naturally my body and my brain don't want to do anything. 
They just want to follow the least resistant path at the moment: if I'm running - stop; if I'm confused solving a math problem - check the solution; if I'm bored - open youtube.

Best Explainatory Theory:

You're in momentum of lazyness, go against it for 30m and build momentum to start working on your important problem.

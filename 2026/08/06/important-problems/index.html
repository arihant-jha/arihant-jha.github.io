Problems

Problems are arranged in an axiomatic manner by their impact on my current life.

1. Should I sleep a little more? (solved)

It’s hard to wake up, brain sometimes give reasons to not, eyes don’t open up, I’m sore from last day’s 10k, sleep a little more - it’s still early.

Based on evidence I’ve collected over 26 years and after reading about a lot of people, I have realized — I’m Lazy and naturally my body and my brain don’t want to do anything. They just want to follow the least resistant path at the moment: if I’m running - stop; if I’m confused solving a math problem - check the solution; if I’m bored - open youtube.

Best Explainatory Theory:

You’re in momentum of lazyness, go against it for 30m and build momentum to start working on your important problem.

2. Should I work from home or go out? (solved)

Best Explainatory Theory:

Evidence suggest, whenever I have this question it’s best to go to the cafe; because I have started becoming lazy and my brain will pour in superstitious theories - it knows when I’m tired or half asleep I might not conjecture it well, but there are 3 possibilites of what can happen next

1/ I fight with my willpower and win, but in this case I waste energy fighting.

2/ I don’t fight and succumb to superstition.

3/ At the instant I get this tought, I run as fast as possible to the cafe to catch opposing momentum 1. Should I sleep a little more? (solved). No energy or time wasted. More chances of getting great work done.

3. Just got an idea when I’m about to sleep, what to do? (solved)

Best Explainatory Theory:

Sleep is easy to come, what’s hard is the idea itself and the inspiration to work on the idea; generally the inspiration is highest when you get the idea. Evidence suggests when I don’t work on it immediately, I can get happy and almost make myself feel I’m there when I’m not; if I have not solved it by the next day - I’ll be pilling up regret; and you know the worst case - I forget the idea! Now I’ll regret both the former and later.

When you get the idea, jump out of bed or whatever you’re doing and put all your effort into making it a reality.

4. I have wasted the day, now there are only 3 hours left, what should I do? (solved)

Fist possibility - You sleep with regret Second possibility - In these 3 hours you may hit a breakthrough and get a new idea or inspiration or both; Remember when you’re inventing a new solution, it’s always about a moment, not an hour, a min, a day - a moment in time; so err towards the possibility which improves the odd for you to have that moment and then make good use of it.
